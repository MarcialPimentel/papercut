## About this game

Paste into **Description → About This Game**. The `[h2]`, `[b]` and `[list]` tags are Steam's own formatting.

```
[h2]A sword duel cut from paper[/h2]
Two papercraft samurai face off inside a pop-up notebook. There are no health numbers: every hit cuts the paper fighter red, slices their paper health strip shorter, and the decisive blow cuts them clean in half.

[h2]The mouse is your sword[/h2]
Move the mouse up or down to guard high or low. Left click for a fast light slash, right click for a slow heavy one. The keyboard moves you: walk, dash and hop.

[h2]Read them. Bait them. Cut them.[/h2]
Feint a heavy to draw out their attack. Parry at the last moment and answer with a riposte. Catch a rival winding up a heavy, or recovering from a miss, and it's a [b]counter cut[/b]: an instant K.O., whatever their health. Every round can turn on one greedy swing.

[h2]A notebook that turns its pages[/h2]
Fight across the Graph Paper Dojo, the Ruled Courtyard and the Legal Pad Grove, with the winner left standing on the marble back cover. Between rounds the paper folds flat and the page turns.

[h2]Ways to fight[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Fight a friend[/b] online with an invite link, and pick the rules: rounds, health and timer.
[*][b]Find a fight[/b] with whoever is waiting.
[*][b]Tournaments[/b] for 3 to 8 friends, with a knockout bracket.
[*][b]Endless:[/b] how many bots can you cut?
[*][b]Training[/b] against a dummy you set up: high or low guard, jumping, walking in or away.
[/list]
```
